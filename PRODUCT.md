# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers, recruiters, collaborators, data practitioners, civic/marketing stakeholders, and technical peers who visit edgarsierra.com to understand Edgar Sierra's work, read analysis, subscribe to updates, or inspect technical projects.

They usually need fast orientation: what Edgar works on, why the work is credible, how the analysis was built, and where to go next.

## Product Purpose

edgarsierra.com is Edgar Sierra's public home base for Spanish-language writing, research, technical projects, and professional credibility.

Success means the site feels rigorous but accessible, makes data work legible without flattening the technical depth, and turns flagship projects into reusable brand assets.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, clear, and grounded.

The voice should feel analytical without becoming academic, personal without becoming casual, and technically serious without reading as corporate.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-startup aesthetics.
- Purple gradients, decorative glassmorphism, and visual effects that overpower the analysis.
- Betting, certainty, or guaranteed-prediction language around probabilistic projects.
- Unattributed or unauthorized internal project details.
- Dense academic presentation that hides the practical point.

## Design Principles

- Make the work inspectable: show data, methods, uncertainty, and limits clearly.
- Lead with trust: every strong claim should have enough context to be evaluated.
- Keep Spanish as the user-facing language across navigation, copy, metadata, and calls to action.
- Treat visual design as a support for comprehension, not decoration.
- Preserve the site's existing design system unless a local experience explicitly earns an exception.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Maintain colorblind-safe color choices, visible focus states, readable type, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts that keep text, tables, and controls usable on mobile.
